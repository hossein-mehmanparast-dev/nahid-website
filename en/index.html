Book

The Magic of Nahid Date

This inspiring and unique book is not just one of thousands of cookbooks that focus only on delicious and varied recipes that nourish the body, but it also includes principles for nutrition and mental well-being

This creative idea led to a path in which products could be introduced to the market in a more coherent and carefully planned way.

Since some of these products may still be unfamiliar to consumers in the target market, the recipes were compiled into a book.

In this book, Nahid brand ingredients are used as a way to introduce the products and provide special instructions on how they can be used. For the first time, this book presents 22 healthy date recipes and 22 golden rules for a happy life based on real and personal experiences, completing the business journey of the Nahid brand.
